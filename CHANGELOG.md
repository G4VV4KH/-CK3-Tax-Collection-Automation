# Tax Collection Automation 0.3.0

For CK3 1.20.1.1.

- Reworked tax automation around income-focused collector selection and taxpayer allocation, including existing assignments.
- Replaced the old monthly review cycle with explicit enablement and relevant tax-state change triggers. Tax decrees remain the player's choice.
- Reduced unnecessary window refreshes and batched safe taxpayer transfers to shorten automated reviews.
- Improved load recovery and handling of stalled read-only previews; actual assignment checks remain mandatory.
- Completed localization for all nine CK3 languages and corrected missing tax-decree requirement text.
- Updated instructions, compatibility information and the explanation of engine-imposed pauses and temporary tax-window control.

Upgrade note: back up your save. Finish any pending exchange with 0.1.x before upgrading; unfinished legacy exchanges are held rather than automatically migrated. Turn automation off and wait for cleanup before removing the mod.
